SETUP
[ ] Use a disposable sandbox and keep the current mod combination recorded.
[ ] Confirm Rearranged S282 1.3.3 is active in Ctrl+F10.
[ ] Have an oil can, enough steam/water/fuel, and track for forward/reverse runs.
[ ] Begin with a stock 2-8-2 and note its rod motion, controls and oilcup positions.
[ ] Record weather, train load, direction and approximate boiler pressure for
    traction comparisons; use similar conditions for each arrangement.
[ ] Before EVERY switch: stop, close regulator, let driving force and slip settle.

Manual testing can change both engines independently. During an isolation check,
leave the other engine untouched so changes to it are easy to spot.
Selections are temporary. Save/reload persistence is not implemented in this trial.
The automated structural test is separate and does not need repeating per row.

PER-ARRANGEMENT CHECKS - USE THESE CODES IN THE MATRIX
Mark P=pass, F=fail, N=not tested. A blank is not a pass.

V - STATIC VISUALS AND CONTROLS
[ ] Correct leading, driving and trailing wheel count on BOTH sides.
[ ] Wheels sit on rails; supports, cylinders and brake shoes are sensibly placed.
[ ] Rods/valve gear are attached, with no duplicate meshes, gaps or major clipping.
[ ] Lubricator, compressor, labels and gauges are in usable positions.
[ ] No leftover fifth/sixth wheels, rods or sparks on a smaller arrangement.

M - MOTION AND BRAKING
[ ] Roll slowly forward and backward: wheels rotate in the right direction.
[ ] Rods stay connected through a full rotation; no jumps, stretching or freezing.
[ ] Crossheads and valve gear move plausibly on both sides.
[ ] Roll through a curve and points; look for unexpected jumps or instability.
[ ] Brake to a stop in both directions; no inexplicable locked/sliding wheels.
[ ] Listen for missing/duplicated chuffs or mechanical sounds; observe steam and
    spark effects during suitable operating conditions. Record which other
    chuff/effects mods are enabled when reporting a problem.

O - OILCUPS (DETAILED SIX-CUP RECORD BELOW)
[ ] Locate all SIX native cups: three on each side of the mechanism.
[ ] Open each cup, refill with the oil can, observe the normal level/refill
    response where visible, and close it. No inaccessible collider or ghost cup.
[ ] Cups follow their associated mechanism during forward and reverse motion.
[ ] No floating, duplicated or detached cups, including on 10/12-coupled engines.
[ ] Switch after stopping: existing oil levels are retained as far as visible;
    no instant empty/full reset caused by changing the arrangement.
[ ] Return to stock and check cup positions and refill operation again.
    Native oil use over elapsed running time is expected; do not treat ordinary
    consumption as a switch reset. There remain six native oiling circuits,
    not an additional circuit for every extra driving axle.

T - LOADED PULL, SLIP AND SANDING
[ ] Start and pull the same manageable train in comparable conditions.
[ ] Power responds normally; no sudden loss, impossible force or runaway RPM.
[ ] Deliberately provoke a brief wheelslip, then close regulator to recover.
[ ] Driver animation and sparks agree with slip; unpowered wheels behave normally.
[ ] Compare sanding on/off under repeatable conditions; no persistent stuck slip.
    Different radii and powered/total axle counts can change pull/slip behaviour.
    Identical performance across arrangements is not the expected pass criterion.

D - DISTANCE, LOD AND RELOAD OF EXTERNAL CONTROLS
[ ] Inspect both sides close up and from progressively farther away.
[ ] Wheels/rods/supports remain coherent when LOD changes.
[ ] Walk far enough away for external controls to unload, then return.
[ ] Six cups and lubricator/compressor controls reappear, follow the mechanism
    and can still be operated/refilled. No duplicate controls or displaced labels.

R - RETURN TO STOCK AND ISOLATION
[ ] Return to stock 2-8-2: original wheel layout, body, rods and controls restored.
[ ] Original oilcup positions/refill controls work and oil levels are retained.
[ ] Compare the untouched second engine: its layout and controls did not change.
