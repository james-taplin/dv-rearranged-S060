REARRANGED S060 0.3.6-mp - MULTIPLAYER CANDIDATE

This candidate ports the current Rearranged S282 multiplayer integration.
It includes the S060 0.3.5 switching, buffer and fixed-sleeve repairs.
Build and offline checks pass. Live multiplayer acceptance awaits James.

SETUP

Every player must use this identical complete ZIP and its normal requirements.
Use DV Multiplayer 0.1.16.0-beta (API 1.1), the donor's supported API target.
Rearranged S282 is not required. Both rearranger mods can be installed together.
Multiplayer supplies MultiplayerAPI.dll. Do not copy that DLL into this mod.
Multiplayer remains optional for single-player use.

USE

Use the S060 Wheel Rearranger on your own radio, or the UMM arrangement buttons.
The host checks each requested change and sends the result to all players.
The host can permit all guests or lock individual guests in the S060 UMM panel.
Locked guests continue to receive arrangements. Random spawn choices use the
host's settings. Each player's saved preferences remain local.
All existing stopped-car and Meyer rear-clearance rules apply on the host.

JAMES'S ACCEPTANCE CHECKS

1. Verify ordinary single-player use with Multiplayer disabled, then with its
   internal single-player mode. There should be no multiplayer lock or status.
2. Host and join with the same ZIP. From each player's radio, select stock,
   short-wheelbase 0-6-0 and each other arrangement. Check identical geometry.
3. Lock all guests, then one guest. Check that requests are refused while
   incoming changes remain visible. Unlock and retry.
4. Try a request while moving or with the regulator open. Check host refusal.
   Try Meyer with the rear coupled, hose connected or track occupied.
5. Drive Meyer and Dockside, including a slipping powered group. Compare each
   group's wheel motion, sparks and sound. Check Shay shafts, wheels and crank
   in both directions, with cylinder cocks open and closed.
6. Join a running session containing rearranged locomotives. Check their
   arrangements, motion and effects, then switch back to stock while stopped.
7. Save/reload on the host and reconnect. Check arrangements, damage, oil and
   Meyer resources. Let a locomotive unload/reload and check it again.
8. If S282 is also installed, check that its radio and permissions stay separate.
   An old or different S060 package must fail the matching-build check.

Also drive a fresh Meyer, stop with the regulator closed, select Dockside and
drive again. Repeat from Shay. Check buffers while coupling and uncoupling.

Record the actual mod versions and both Player.log files for any failure.
For 0-4-0, check that the middle wheel, calipers and brake shoes are absent.
Change back to stock and check that the middle wheel and brakes return.
For 0-10-0, 0-8-0 and short-wheelbase 0-6-0, check the fitted brakes on both
sides while moving and braking. Check heat glow and cooling, then change back
to stock and check restoration. Save and reload the fitted arrangements.
Offline fixtures do not establish live transport, rail dynamics, audible sound,
real saved-world acceptance or coexistence with another running multiplayer mod.

Check every arrangement for floating or duplicate oil cups on both sides.
On stock/short 0-6-0, 0-4-0, 0-8-0, 0-10-0 and 0-6-2, the guide cups should
remain seated on the upper slidebars. Added big-end cups should follow their
native coupling-rod bosses. Check at rest and while moving slowly.
Check Meyer and Dockside cups through curves and the Shay cups on its bearings
and off-side shelves. Confirm oil-can refill, lid controls and saved oil levels.
On 0-4-0, 0-8-0 and 0-10-0, check the native rod profile at near/far distances,
then restore stock and reload to check that no old cups or rods remain.
