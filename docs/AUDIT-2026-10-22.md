# Rearranged S060 code audit — 22 October 2026

This report's original findings and baseline below concern **0.13.1**. The
appended **0.14.0 and 0.15.0 resolution records** describe subsequent changes; read them
before treating a historical defect or test total as the current status.

## Verdict and baseline

**Fix the lifecycle defects before release or additional arrangements. A wholesale
rewrite is not justified.** The production implementation has useful ownership
boundaries and substantial numerical coverage, but scene initialization, detached
interiors and failure/destruction paths are less well tested. The latest chimney
correction is incomplete despite its passing isolated reader audit.

Scope: current dirty working tree, runtime **0.13.1**, Meyer/Dockside packs
**0.6.0**, B99 build2702, .NET Framework4.8, Unity2019.4.40f1. HEAD is
`5806ef70ea3bc2e9b9ce573948119e7eac0806ae`; HEAD alone does **not** reproduce the
working tree. Many current implementation files are still untracked. Preserve
all of them; do not clean/reset this checkout.

Three agents reviewed simulation/saves/oiling, lifecycle/effects/radio, and
geometry/physics/authoring. The parent reviewed build selection, packaging,
test coverage and cross-checked the main findings against source/native code.
Stage7 evaluates to54 compile items including the publicizer helper; the default
stage evaluates to108. Inherited S282 implementation is not active in Stage7.

Audit operations: source/configuration/serialized evidence inspection, native
implementation comparisons, builds and existing headless tests. No production
source, asset, installed mod, settings or save changes. Generated build/test
outputs were refreshed. This report is the only new source-tree document.

## Prioritized defects

### F01 — High: initial chimney reader misses exhaust-owner registration

**Confirmed source-order defect; no live game reproduction in this audit.**

`RearrangedS060/S060ArticulatedSimulation.cs:60–69` creates `Exhaust` and registers
the flow owner only in the native `SimController.Initialize` postfix. Native
`SimController.Initialize` calls `particlesController.Init(simFlow)` inside its
original method first (`build/meyer/native-source/SimController.cs:140–142`).
`S060ExhaustPresentation.cs:65–70` therefore sees no owner and skips the initial
chimney reader. Its `Refresh` at42–48 only visits already enrolled readers.

**Effect:** the initial Meyer chimney stays bound to HP; the Dockside chimney
does not gain its second unit's pulse source. Later initialized audio can still
be routed correctly. Do not claim the current installed chimney fix is complete.

**Small fix:** enroll already initialized native chimney readers after the owner
is published, then retain the existing hooks for subsequent readers. Preserve
native particle baselines when rebinding. Do not initialize the whole sim again.

**Regression:** initialize smoke before owner registration, then publish owner,
select Meyer/Dockside/stock and assert exact event and pressure subscriptions.
The existing audit registers the owner first, masking this case
(`Meyer/Authoring/ExhaustNativeAudit.cs`, owner registration before smoke Init).

### F02 — Medium: detached native interior audio is missed

**Confirmed hierarchy mismatch; audible effect not listened to in this audit.**

`S060StockCocks.cs:24–28` scans only beneath `car`. Native `TrainCar.SetupInterior`
detaches the interior (`build/meyer/native-source/TrainCar.cs:1245`); native
`TrainAudio.SetupForCar` parents pooled audio beneath that detached interior.
The stock cock reader is therefore outside the mute scan and remains alongside
the articulated unit readers.

The same assumption affects translated cock/crack sound placement in
`S060CylinderFittings.cs:32–36` and `S060NativeCylinders.cs:26`. These are one
discovery/ownership problem, not three independent defects.

**Small fix:** include both native car and detached interior roots, deduplicate
readers, and handle audio arriving after conversion through native lifecycle
registration. Restore original IDs/poses when returning to stock or streaming out.

**Regression:** a detached interior and pooled reader; articulated selection
must leave exactly the intended two cock sources, then restore stock. Repeat
stream-out/in and moved-cylinder sound-position checks.

### F03 — Medium: Meyer collision boxes do not follow the articulated rear shell

**Confirmed transform mismatch; gameplay collision effect not live reproduced.**

`S060MeyerSupport.cs:84–94` creates rear deck/pannier/beam BoxColliders under the
fixed car collision root. `S060Articulated.cs:83–89` parents visible rear geometry
under the yawing native rear bogie. No collider pose synchronization follows.
At12 degrees, a1.525m deck half-length yields roughly0.32m lateral disagreement.

**Small fix:** make the rear collision group follow the rear frame pose while
retaining the intended native rigidbody ownership. Preserve stock restoration.

**Regression:** compare visible shell and collision corners at0/±12/±15 degrees,
including rerail and stock restoration. A mesh-only preview cannot establish this.

### F04 — Medium: oil topology depends on successful visual/interactable construction

**Confirmed failure-path coupling; resulting hidden-circuit damage is likely.**

`S060OilCups.cs:72–90` clears cups and asynchronously rebuilds them. Only successful
completion at181 calls `S060Oiling.ConfigureActive`. A material/provider/native-cup
discovery exception clears geometry and logs an error but leaves the previous
arrangement's active mask unchanged. It can remain wrong indefinitely.

**Trigger:** change080 to stock with an empty extra oil circuit and a failed cup
rebuild. The extra circuit can remain active although its axle/cup is absent.
The three-frame deferred update also creates a shorter stale-mask window on
successful changes.

**Small fix:** derive active circuit topology synchronously from the arrangement;
cosmetic/interactive cup construction must not decide whether native damage
circuits exist. Surface servicing readiness separately.

**Regression:** inject a cup-discovery failure after switching a worn four-axle
layout to stock; tick native oiling and confirm hidden points neither consume
nor contribute damage. Check state/lids remain preserved for later reuse.

### F05 — Medium: direct destruction bypasses conventional owned-mesh disposal

**Confirmed cleanup gap; retained memory and collection timing unmeasured.**

`S060Lifecycle.cs:92–94` invokes `S060Conversion.ForgetDestroyed`; the latter at178
only drops the dictionary entry. Actual restoration/disposal is in
`S060Conversion.cs:158–175`. Native direct-deletion paths bypass ReturnCarToPool.

Conventional rod/body-pipe owners are plain IDisposable objects, not components
with OnDestroy. `S060RodMesh.cs:54–66` creates a runtime Mesh and destroys it only
on disposal; `S060CouplingRods` and `S060CylinderFittings` hold such owners.
Destroying a MeshFilter does not explicitly release these owned mesh objects.
Articulated SteamPipes has its own destruction cleanup and is not the same case.

**Small fix:** a common pre-destruction cleanup or resource-only disposal path.
Do not attempt full physics restoration after Unity hierarchy destruction.

**Regression:** direct-delete converted040/080 cars without pooling, repeat, and
compare owned Mesh counts; also test ordinary pool reuse and scene unload.

### F06 — Medium: ordinary build and legacy package entry points select the wrong product

**Confirmed by MSBuild evaluation and script inspection.**

`RearrangedS060.csproj` defaults Stage to1 and Version to0.1.0. The solution has
no release configuration selecting Stage7. `Build.Functional.ps1:3–14` builds
Stage4 (DLL0.5.0), then copies current Info.json (0.13.1) and names the zip from
that metadata. Older Meyer/Dockside scripts likewise retain older runtime paths.
The recommended Stage7 pipeline is correct; alternative paths are misleading.

**Small fix:** make one explicit current build/package entry point and move old
spikes into an unmistakable historical area. If legacy scripts remain runnable,
generate matching metadata and reject incompatible pack combinations.

**Regression:** ordinary solution build and every advertised package command
must identify the intended runtime and agree on DLL/Info/zip versions.

### F07 — Medium: release build relies on ignored historical local evidence

**Confirmed reproducibility defect, not a failure of today's populated checkout.**

`Build.Radio.ps1:7` requires an old0.13.0 cock report and line32 requires its
native-preview fixture. Both live beneath ignored build/. Scripts also embed
machine-specific tool/game paths, and current source contains untracked release
files. A clean checkout cannot run the advertised release pipeline as it stands.

**Small fix:** provide a documented prepare/validate command that regenerates
local-only native fixtures and reports from an installed game. Centralize paths
and version selection; retain native assets outside version control. Record all
required source in version control when the user chooses to commit.

**Regression:** reproduce the candidate from a clean checkout plus a documented
local game/tool configuration, without copying a previous build directory.

### F08 — Medium: asset-pack validation is not bound to the copied bundle hash

**Confirmed validation gap; no evidence today's installed bundles were altered.**

`Package.Articulated.ps1:19–29` accepts passed JSON reports and matching pack
ID/version, then copies ccl_bundle. It does not verify that this binary is the
same binary those reports validated. Replacing/editing a bundle in an evidence
directory can silently carry stale green reports into a new package. Hashing the
finished package proves later integrity, not validation provenance.

**Small fix:** record the reopened bundle's SHA256 in validation/export receipts
and verify it before packaging. Apply the same source-receipt rule to core
metadata/credits rather than validating only the DLL.

**Regression:** alter a bundle byte after validation and require packaging to
reject it before staging/installing. This is especially useful during future
authoring iterations where filenames and version numbers remain unchanged.

## Maintainability findings — separate from runtime defects

- **M01: split S060OilCups by responsibility.** It currently combines topology,
  prefab cloning/remapping, native controller setup, delayed initialization,
  mount fitting and teardown. F04 demonstrates why topology must be separate.
  Three small collaborators are sufficient; no new service framework is needed.
- **M02: centralize stage/unit mapping.** `steamEngine` means physical front HP
  on Meyer but a different physical unit on Dockside; `rs060FrontEngine` is the
  rear LP Meyer unit. Keep stable saved IDs but expose named HP/LP and physical
  front/rear mappings so presentation code does not reconstruct this repeatedly.
- **M03: explicit engine-save schema.** S060EngineState reflects native private
  cylinder float/bool fields into a fixed-version save. Native field changes can
  silently change the schema; repeated uncached reflection also obscures which
  values matter. Use a checked, cached field map and explicit migration policy.
- **M04: one calibration source.** Compound cycle construction, native stage
  definitions, standalone CCL setup and test fixtures repeat bore/stroke/volume/
  flow values. Share immutable calibration data; tests should not independently
  reconstruct the production graph and thereby miss its wiring/order mistakes.
- **M05: enforce the claimed transaction boundary.** SwitchTo's preflight does
  not check donor packs before dismantling the previous conversion. Several
  rollback paths log cleanup failures and lose the temporary owner. Factor the
  existing donor validation into preflight and test injected apply/undo errors.
  Do not describe the current failure behavior as unconditionally all-or-nothing.
- **M06: separate historical code from supported paths.** Inherited S282 code is
  excluded from Stage7, so it is not shipping runtime spaghetti. Keeping it beside
  live S060 sources and several old package commands still increases navigation
  and maintenance risk. Archive intentionally after references/builds are mapped.
- **M07: report scenarios, not only assertion totals.** Transitions/Program.cs
  repeats one getter fixture100 times. Geometry sweeps and engine switch loops
  also contribute many checks. These loops are useful but are not100 independent
  lifecycle scenarios or proof of integrated native initialization.

## Recommended cleanup order

1. Add realistic startup-order/detached-interior regressions and fix F01/F02.
2. Decouple oil circuit topology and repair direct-destruction ownership (F04/F05).
3. Make Meyer collision follow the visible rear section and validate curves (F03).
4. Consolidate supported build/package commands and hash-bound evidence (F06–F08).
5. Extract oiling responsibilities, semantic stage mappings and shared calibration;
   introduce an explicit save schema without changing existing saved identities.
6. Archive obsolete source/build routes, then establish a clean-checkout build.
7. Complete hot/cold audio, oil-can/lid, lamp, collision, pooling, rerail and
   save/reload gameplay acceptance before calling this release-ready.

Keep each fix small and reviewable. Add new arrangements only after these
lifecycle boundaries are covered. No broad rewrite, extra DLL or custom
replacement for native game systems is supported by the audit evidence.

## 0.14.0 resolution record — 22 October 2026

The following source fixes address this audit. Final candidate test receipts and
installed state belong in [HANDOVER.md](HANDOVER.md); this record does not claim
that a complete in-game acceptance run occurred. Source inspection and individual
development runs are distinguished from final package validation.

### Runtime defects

- **F01 addressed:** `S060ExhaustPresentation.EnrollInitialized` discovers native
  smoke/chuff readers already initialized before owner registration. It rebinds
  only initialized, previously unenrolled matching readers, preserving the smoke
  baseline. Existing hooks handle later Init/Deinit. The regression now creates
  smoke before publishing the owner, matching native startup order.
- **F02 addressed:** `S060NativeAudio` discovers beneath both the car and detached
  interior and deduplicates readers. Stock cock presentation mutes both control
  and flow while articulated sources own the effect. Native TrainAudio
  SetupForCar/ReturnToPool hooks place and restore moved cylinder emitters,
  including audio loaded after conversion. The audio controller itself remains
  in the native pool hierarchy. Audits use detached pooled TrainAudio rather
  than assuming everything is beneath TrainCar. Audible balance remains a
  gameplay check.
- **F03 addressed:** `S060MeyerCollision` synchronizes the rear collision group's
  pose from the actual rear frame in FixedUpdate and LateUpdate. Colliders remain
  beneath the native car collision root, preserving the car Rigidbody rather
  than becoming part of the bogie Rigidbody. Restore disables the group
  immediately before deferred destruction. Controlled tests compare collider
  corners/body ownership through yaw, rerail/world transforms and disposal;
  integrated driving/contact remains a gameplay check.
- **F04 addressed:** `S060OilingTopology` owns stable per-arrangement circuit
  indices/counts. Conversion commit, stock restoration, release and stock
  no-conversion paths select the mask synchronously, before delayed servicing
  geometry. Cup construction never changes it; `ServicingReady` is separate.
  New stock flows immediately enable the two additional big ends per side.
  **501 native assertions passed** during development, including 0-8-0 -> Stock
  with no visual owner for the initial deferred frames and an indefinite failed
  build window, all five dormant port values, reactivation, native reload and
  every circuit across all six arrangements. Existing circuit IDs, quantities,
  lids and dormant saved values are retained.
- **F05 addressed:** native pre-destruction cleanup attempts normal release while
  components are intact. The last-resort destruction path removes dictionary
  ownership and releases owned conventional/articulated presentation meshes
  without attempting late physics restoration. Disposal is idempotent.
  The development native cleanup fixture passed **109 assertions** across direct
  deletion, partial teardown and normal release. It does not establish a
  long-session memory profile or scene-unload behaviour in the full game.

### Build and evidence defects

- **F06 addressed:** the project and low-level build helper now default to Stage
  7. `Build.Runtime.ps1` is the current DLL command; `Build.Release.ps1` is the
  tested complete-package command. Obsolete prototype package entry points stop
  with an explicit replacement message. Packaging verifies the runtime assembly
  version against current metadata. Historical source is retained for reference,
  not advertised as another supported release route.
- **F07 addressed in tooling:** `Build.Config.ps1` centralizes local game/tool
  paths and reads the version from `Info.json`. Optional ignored `Build.Local.json`
  overrides machine paths. `Prepare.QA.ps1` regenerates local-only native fixtures
  and records input/output hashes instead of requiring a previous 0.13.x build
  directory. `Build.Release.ps1` prepares the authoring project as needed, exports
  both packs, runs runtime/geometry checks and stages the package. See
  [BUILDING.md](../BUILDING.md). A clean-clone reproduction is **not yet proven**:
  the working tree still contains untracked implementation files and must be
  committed before a clone can contain the complete source.
- **F08 addressed:** the Unity reopening audit writes the actual bundle SHA256.
  `Complete.BundleEvidence.ps1` binds that binary, pack metadata and report hashes;
  `Test.BundleEvidence.ps1` requires the exact canonical report set and checks
  paths, hashes, passed results and the reopened binary. Package staging verifies
  every core-receipt file, not just the DLL. Native runtime report hashes are also
  bound to the exact DLL and must include the startup/detached-audio regressions.
  Final packaging must run these checks; version labels alone are insufficient.

### Maintainability follow-up

- **M01 partly addressed:** topology is extracted from `S060OilCups`; prefab
  cloning/controller registration and mount fitting remain together. No service
  framework or replacement oiling simulation was introduced.
- **M02 remains:** stable saved slot names are retained. Further semantic
  front/rear/HP/LP naming cleanup is separate from these defect fixes.
- **M03 addressed:** `S060EngineState` uses a cached checked map of eleven native
  engine fields and six cylinder fields, retaining version 1 and exact JSON
  names/order. Native field type/set changes fail with an explicit compatibility
  message, requiring deliberate migration. **223 native engine assertions passed**
  in development, including byte-for-byte comparison with the old serializer,
  legacy round trips and atomic malformed-cylinder rejection. Conservation and
  traction checks remained unchanged.
- **M04 partly addressed:** production cycle/stage creation and native-bank test
  fixtures share `S060SteamCalibration`, retaining the old double/float precision
  and tuning. Authoring/proxy definitions still contain their own calibration;
  standalone Meyer runtime intentionally reads its CCL graph dimensions. Further
  authoring consolidation needs shared-source wiring and fresh export validation.
- **M05 partly addressed:** donor pack/frame preflight now occurs before removing
  the previous conversion. Failure/rollback cleanup has improved. Arbitrary
  injected undo-failure transaction guarantees have not been demonstrated;
  do not claim unconditional all-or-nothing recovery.
- **M06 partly addressed:** misleading prototype package commands are retired.
  Inherited source remains in place, excluded from Stage 7. Archiving the source
  tree itself is still a separate navigation cleanup.
- **M07 applied to reporting:** assertion counts are labelled as repeated checks,
  not independent scenes. The native regressions now include the concrete startup,
  detached-audio, failed-servicing and direct-destruction scenarios previously
  missing. Their scope still stops short of full gameplay acceptance.

Remaining acceptance: first-load hot/cold exhaust, detached/streamed audio,
oil-can/lids, Meyer collision in curves/rerail, pool/delete reuse, coupled saves
and a longer-session performance check. Nothing in this resolution record
establishes later-game-build or multiplayer compatibility.

## 0.15.0 cleanup record — 22 October 2026

  orchestration. `S060OilCupInteractables` owns native prefab cloning, component
