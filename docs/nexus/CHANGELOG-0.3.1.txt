Version 0.3.1 - staged independent-wheelslip candidate

- separate powered sets can now slip independently on Dockside and Meyer,
  using native DV adhesion and slip calculations with individual engine RPM
- reusable group traction maps each engine to its actual driven axles and
  applies each group's limited force once; spare grip is no longer pooled
- rods, crank phase, sparks and slip audio follow the relevant powered set
- Meyer HP and LP steam calculations now accept their own wheel speeds while
  retaining the finite receiver, backpressure and conserved steam/energy flow
- corrected hot-reload intake temperature preparation exposed by unequal HP/LP
  speed testing, without changing old save IDs or the compound calibration
- safe release/restoration on arrangement switching and native teardown;
  switching waits for both powered sets to settle
- the shaft-linked Shay remains one drive; native braking/wheel-slide and
  global cab slip indications remain car-wide

Offline validation and gameplay limits are recorded in docs/HANDOVER.md.
Staged only: not installed or published. James performs DV driving acceptance.
