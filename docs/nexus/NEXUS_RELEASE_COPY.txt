MOD NAME
Rearranged S060 - Re:Arranged

VERSION / FILE CATEGORY
0.22.2 - Meyer skin, coal and pipework / Main file

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Give the S060 new wheel arrangements, a compound Meyer with extra tanks over a
swinging rear engine, a two-truck Shay or a small-driver Franklin Dockside. Change layouts with the radio,
with animated running gear, working oil cups and cab-controlled cylinder cocks.

DESCRIPTION PAGE - NEXUS BBCODE

[b]Rearranged S060 - Re:Arranged[/b]

Give Derail Valley's S060 a different character: a compact four-coupled engine,
a short-wheelbase six-coupled layout, an eight-coupled tank, a 0-6-2, a ten-coupled tank, a compound
Meyer-style engine, a two-truck Shay or a small-driver Franklin Dockside.

Rearranged S060 is a fork of my Rearranged S282 B99 / Re:Arranged fork,
itself based on Peter Schinske / SatiricRug's original Rearranged S282. The fork
retains the original MIT licence and copyright notices.

[b]Nine arrangement choices[/b]
[list]
[*]0-6-0 Stock - original 1.42 m drivers.
[*]0-4-0 - four-coupled layout with stock-sized drivers.
[*]0-6-0 Short Wheelbase - closer axle spacing with fitted 1.14 m drivers.
[*]0-8-0 - four powered axles using the same fitted 1.14 m drivers.
[*]0-10-0 Tank - five powered axles with fitted 1.00 m drivers.
[*]0-6-2 - stock-sized drivers with a trailing carrying axle.
[*]Meyer-style 0-4+4-0T Compound - two driven axles beneath the S060 and two on a
swinging rear engine unit under extra tanks and a coal hopper, sharing the same cab, locomotive identity and save.
[*]Franklin Dockside 0-4-4-0T - two simple-expansion units, 1.00 m drivers and
animated Franklin gear, all beneath one tank locomotive.
[*]Two-truck Shay - three vertical cylinders, jointed drive shafts, bevel gears
and four driven axles on stock S282 pony wheelsets.
[/list]

[b]Two-truck Shay[/b]
A fictional S060 conversion with a three-cylinder side engine and 43:19 geared
drive to two swivelling trucks. Steam and exhaust pipes connect the engine to
the boiler and single chimney. All three cylinder-cock channels use the cab
lever; six upright oil cups serve the shaft bearings, truck frames and crank. The Shay is
available from the radio and UMM chooser, and is excluded from random spawning.
This first version still needs gameplay acceptance, especially on tight curves.

[b]Meyer compound[/b]
The Meyer has an HP engine, finite steam receiver and LP engine. Its LP exhaust
and chuffs feed the locomotive's single chimney, with a jointed return pipe routed
low along the cab roof and left tank top. Bodywork behind the cab adds a high coal
hopper, side and well tanks and a rear end carrying all three rear lamps; the rear
engine unit swings underneath. The Meyer carries about 8,800 L of water and
3,500 kg of coal (stock 4,500 L and 1,200 kg). This is a fictional Meyer-inspired
arrangement, rather than a replica of a particular historical locomotive.

[b]Franklin Dockside[/b]
Both powered units receive boiler steam directly. Animated Franklin shafts,
fitted rods, cylinders and connected steam pipes stay under the S060 body. Both
units contribute exhaust and chuffs to one chimney. There is no rear appendage
or separate companion vehicle.

[b]Working details[/b]
[list]
[*]Dedicated WHEEL REARRANGER radio mode and an arrangement chooser in UMM.
[*]Optional random arrangements for newly spawned S060s, using a selectable pool.
[*]Retained locomotive identity, livery, cab, fuel, water, damage and save ownership.
[*]Independent articulated engine motion and effects, with both sets of cylinder
cocks controlled by the existing cab lever.
[*]Two functional big-end oil cups per driven axle, alongside the original guide/
crosshead lubricators. Oil quantities and lid states persist across layout changes.
[*]Fixed wheel sizes fitted to each arrangement; full-sized native front cylinders
on the Meyer and live S060 material references.
[*]Meyer LP sound tuning informed by discorddraconequus's Verde compound.
[/list]

[b]Requirements and installation[/b]
Requires [b]Derail Valley B99 on Windows[/b], [b]Unity Mod Manager 0.27.3+[/b],
[url=https://www.nexusmods.com/derailvalley/mods/867]Locomotive Mesh Splitter[/url]
and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/derailvalley/mods/324]Custom Car Loader[/url].
Install CCL's own requirements too, including DV Language Helper for the tested
CCL 3.1.11. These dependencies are separate downloads. Rearranged S282 is not
required to play this mod.

Close the game, then drag [b]RearrangedS060-0.22.2-UMM.zip directly into UMM[/b].
One Rearranged S060 entry includes the runtime and both Meyer/Dockside bundles.
There are no ZIPs inside the download and no separate asset-pack installs.

If you used an earlier three-part test build, first uninstall the old standalone
RS060_Meyer_0440 and RS060_Franklin_Dockside_0440 entries through UMM. Keep
RearrangedS060 itself and Settings.xml. This one-time step prevents duplicate
asset IDs; future updates use the single ZIP normally. New users skip this step.

Select WHEEL REARRANGER on the radio and point at a native S060. Stop and close
the regulator before switching. Before entering or leaving Meyer, uncouple its
rear, disconnect the hose and leave clear track behind. Use the S060 radio for
normal play; the asset packs' CCL spawner entries are debug asset donors.

[b]Initial beta notes[/b]
The new fifth-wheel state uses mod save version 3. Version 0.22.2 reads earlier
saves; older mod versions cannot restore the new 0-10-0 arrangement.

The latest collision correction passes automatic native and contact checks.
The reported stopped Meyer bob and Meyer-to-Dockside window damage still need
gameplay confirmation after that correction. Report repeats with the exact
transition, Player.log and the mod's automatic diagnostic files. Existing damage
is retained; switching layouts does not repair the locomotive.

Rear LP cock jets can be weak when receiver pressure is low, and drips require
water in the cylinders. The new 0-10-0 requires in-game acceptance testing, particularly on tight curves.
Original brake-shoe meshes are hidden on converted
running gear where they do not fit. The 0-8-0 and 0-6-2 remain experimental.

- the Meyer's rear tanks, hopper and bulkhead now use the applied S060 skin's own
  tank-side texture instead of flat paint.
- filled the Meyer's hopper with the stock tender's coal pile.
- rerouted the Meyer's LP exhaust low along the cab roof and the left tank top instead
  of arching over the cab and hanging outside the tank.

0.22.1 CHANGELOG - PLAIN TEXT

- fixed the Meyer's rear buffers: the buffer beam is back where the buffer stems meet
  their heads.
- stood the Meyer's rear lamps fully proud of the rear bulkhead.
- added deep riveted frame sills to the Shay so there is no gap between the frame and
  the trucks.

0.22.0 CHANGELOG - PLAIN TEXT

- rebuilt the Meyer's rear as bodywork on the locomotive: a high coal hopper
  behind the cab feeding the bunker, long side and well tanks matching the S060's
  tank line, and a rear end with steps, ladders and handrails.
- the rear engine unit now swings underneath instead of carrying the tanks.
- moved the upper rear lamp to the new rear end with the lower pair.
- the Meyer carries about 8,800 L of water and 3,500 kg of coal (stock 4,500 L
  and 1,200 kg); switching back to stock keeps up to the stock capacity.

0.21.0 CHANGELOG - PLAIN TEXT

- rebuilt the Shay on smaller stock S282 pony wheels, lowering both trucks.
- made the line shafts, universal joints, bevel gears, rods, pistons and crank
  full size, with visible gear teeth.
- moved the engine down and inboard so its crankshaft lines up with the line
  shafts.
- kept the S060's own frame and springs above the trucks instead of cutting
  them away, so the boiler underside and firebox are closed.
- replaced the Shay's oil cups with six upright cups: three on the gear-side
  shaft bearings, two on the off-side truck frames and one on the crank.

0.20.3 CHANGELOG - PLAIN TEXT

MAIN FILE DESCRIPTION

Shay rebuild and Meyer rear update for B99. Includes RearrangedS060 0.22.2, original Shay
models and both required 0.7.0 asset bundles in one UMM mod folder. Drag this ZIP directly into
UMM; no nested ZIPs or separate pack installs. Requires Loco Mesh Splitter and Custom Car Loader separately.
Rearranged S282 is not required. See the description's beta notes before use.

INCLUDED COMPONENTS - NOT SEPARATE DOWNLOADS

RearrangedS060/RearrangedS060.dll
Main runtime: S060 radio/UMM choices, arrangement switching, steam simulation,
effects integration, oiling and persistence. The two bundles are already included.

RearrangedS060/assets/Meyer/ccl_bundle

RearrangedS060/assets/Dockside/ccl_bundle

RearrangedS060/assets/Shay/shay_running_gear
