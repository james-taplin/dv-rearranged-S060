REARRANGED S060 0.3.4

Change the S060 wheel arrangement through WHEEL REARRANGER on the comms radio.
You can also use the arrangement menu in Unity Mod Manager.

Choose stock 0-6-0, 0-4-0, short-wheelbase 0-6-0, 0-6-2, 0-8-0 or 0-10-0 Tank.
The mod also includes Meyer, Franklin Dockside and two-truck Shay arrangements.
The original locomotive keeps its cab, paint, identity, damage and saved state.

The Meyer and Dockside powered groups can slip separately.
The Shay uses a three-cylinder engine and 4.3:1 gearing to drive both trucks.
The Meyer adds rear water tanks, a coal hopper and a heavy pinned connection.
The 0-4-0 adds two air reservoirs and their fittings.

The ZIP includes all Meyer, Dockside and Shay parts.
The spawner hides the separate Meyer and Dockside asset entries.
Install it directly with Unity Mod Manager. No separate asset-pack installation is necessary.

REQUIREMENTS

Derail Valley B99 for Windows
Unity Mod Manager 0.27.3 or later
Loco Mesh Splitter
Custom Car Loader and its requirements, including DV Language Helper

Rearranged S282 is not required.

Loco Mesh Splitter:
https://www.nexusmods.com/derailvalley/mods/867

Custom Car Loader:
https://www.nexusmods.com/derailvalley/mods/324

INSTALLATION

1. Close Derail Valley.
2. Install the requirements.
3. Remove separate RS060_Meyer_0440 or RS060_Franklin_Dockside_0440 entries from UMM, if present.
4. Keep Rearranged S060 and its Settings.xml file.
5. Drag RearrangedS060-0.3.4-UMM.zip into the UMM Mods tab.
6. Make sure that version 0.3.4 is enabled.
7. Start the game.

USE

Before you change the arrangement, stop the S060.
Close the regulator.
Before you select or leave Meyer, disconnect the rear coupler.
Before you select or leave Meyer, disconnect the rear brake hose.
Keep the track clear behind the locomotive.
Select WHEEL REARRANGER.
Point the radio at the S060.
Select the required arrangement.

The Meyer holds 8,800 litres of water and 3,500 kilograms of coal.
When you leave Meyer, the mod keeps up to 4,500 litres of water and 1,200 kilograms of coal.
The mod discards the excess.
Changes to the arrangement do not repair damage.

The ZIP contains installation instructions, the release history, credits and the license.
