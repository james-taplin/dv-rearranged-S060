0.23.3 - changes since public 0.22.2-beta

- Mounted the Meyer's guide rails to both powered frames, fitted inboard spring
  seats and hangers, removed redundant stock suspension/support meshes, and
  removed unused return cranks while retaining the native main crank journals.

- Closed the Meyer's firebox-base side gaps with fixed mesh cheek plates,
  hiding exposed fire particles while preserving cylinder/pipe clearance.

- Corrected the inward Meyer's front fit: moved full-size cylinders further
  cabward, fitted longer main rods and guides, retired redundant upper steam
  pipework and equipped the vacated front platform with air-brake machinery.
- Removed the obsolete stock front hanger drop and fixed slide bars from the
  Meyer layout; stock meshes and guides return when switching away.

- Revised the Meyer with inward-facing cylinder groups, correctly phased front
  running gear, a measured 65 mm rearward shift of the rear power unit, and
  connected articulated steam pipework. Compound steam and save IDs are retained.

- Fixed Meyer servicing stopping at stock limits: the original S060 filling
  points now offer the combined 8,800 L water / 3,500 kg coal capacity. Cached
  station limits also return to stock when leaving Meyer.

- Reduced repeated axle-cache allocations and unnecessary tiny-motion steam-pipe
  mesh rebuilds. Invalid pipe poses recover without repeated exception spam.
- Hardened exhaust cleanup after partial initialization, repeated setup and
  teardown failures, preventing lingering listeners or extra exhaust readers.
- Corrected oil-cup counts and seating on conventional, Meyer and Dockside layouts:
  two functional cups per driven axle, with the stock 0-6-0 returning to six cups.
  The geared Shay retains its six upright servicing cups.
- Moved the Shay's two off-side oil cups onto supported sill shelves for access.
- Extended and closed the Shay frame sills to the front buffer beam, added a
  headstock and deep cross stretcher, and corrected finish restoration on reload.
- Replaced the Meyer's hopper coal appearance with a dark brown tarpaulin,
  lowered the upper rear lamp below the bulkhead top, and added walkable/item
  support to the rear tanks and hopper.
- Corrected the Shay wheel-size description to about 0.71 m using the same
  rolling-radius datum as the existing physics.
- Updated installation, feature and compatibility documentation.

Existing features retained from 0.22.2-beta include the two-truck Shay, Meyer R2
rear bodywork, 8,800 L water / 3,500 kg coal capacity and low LP exhaust routing.
These are not newly introduced by 0.23.3. Leaving Meyer retains up to stock
capacity (4,500 L / 1,200 kg); excess is discarded with a log message.

Compatibility: supported saves remain readable. Arrangement IDs, save schemas,
oil circuits, steam calibration and physical wheel sizes are unchanged. One ZIP
installs directly through UMM, with one mod folder and no nested ZIPs. Requires
Custom Car Loader and Loco Mesh Splitter separately; S282 is not required.

Offline checks do not establish cab FPS or gameplay acceptance. James performs
DV testing. This candidate awaits gameplay acceptance before publication.
