# Meyer 0.4.0 test

Use S060 runtime **0.11.0** and Meyer assets **0.4.0**. Select Meyer using the
native S060 radio/UMM. This remains one identity, two axles under the locomotive
and two under its attached rear pannier section. Other arrangements remove
that extension. Dockside has no rear section.

Check the new period-style rear frame, axleboxes, springs, brake rigging, tank
straps/rivets, steps and handrails. Surfaces alias live S060 materials. Panniers
still add no water capacity. This is a fictional locomotive, not a class replica.

The front/rear manifolds and transfer pipes reuse the S282 custom shapes. Watch
both connections while the units swivel on a gentle curve. Both cylinder-cock
sets must follow the same cab lever; allow the rear LP to charge from HP before
expecting discharge. Closing regulator should allow receiver rundown, without
fresh boiler steam being sent straight to LP. Both stages retain cylinder water,
crack/damage, crank phase and receiver state through saves and dormant modes.

Follow [the radio test steps](../docs/RADIO-ARTICULATED-TESTING.md) for identity,
resources, full-size stock cylinders, all fixed arrangements, coupled save
loading and stock restoration. Stop on clear straight track with rear uncoupled
and hose disconnected before adding/removing the extension.

Current offline preview images use transient local native body/wheel/atlas
fixtures. No native assets are bundled; cab interiors/doors and live steam/audio
playback are not shown. Game driving, coupling and playback remain acceptance
checks. Preserve Player.log and UMM-Log.txt after testing.
