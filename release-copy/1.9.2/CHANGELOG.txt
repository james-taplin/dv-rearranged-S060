REARRANGED S282 - B99 CHANGELOG

1.9.2 (since 1.3.6 Beta 2)

New arrangements
- added the 2-6-0 Mogul and 2-4-0.
- added an experimental 2-2-2 with giant, adjustable drivers.
- added the 4-14-4 (seven coupled drivers).
- added the 2-6-6-2 Mallet.
- added the 0-8-8-0 "Angus" and the 2-6-8-0 Great Northern Mallets.
- added the 2-2-2-2-2 and 0-2-2-2-0 triplexes (three single-driver Franklin engines).

Restored from the original mod
- restored the 2-4-4-2 and 4-4-4-4, now as true articulated engines whose front engine swings on curves.
- restored the "4-0-4 Drivers not found" novelty.
- restored burnt/exploded materials on added running gear.

Driving and physics
- articulated layouts run extra native steam engines, each with its own chuffs, cylinder cocks, drips, wear and save state.
- grip now follows the layout: 2-, 4- and 6-coupled engines carry realistic axle loads instead of the whole 111 t loco.
- long rigid wheelbases now drag and squeal on tight curves (can be turned off in settings).

Visuals
- polished the Walschaerts valve gear on the 4-6-2 and 4-6-4.
- bigger triplex drivers, with shorter engines, cylinders further forward and the trailing truck further back.
- more room around the 4-14-4's drivers: drivers moved back with longer rods, trailing truck moved back.
- cylinder-cock steam and drips now come from the cylinders on every layout.
- fixed missing cylinders, stray frame pieces and level-of-detail glitches on articulated layouts.

Fixes
- fixed the front coupling chain and hoses being pushed into the frame after conversion.
- fixed silent brake release and missing rolling-sound clicks on converted engines.

Settings and tools
- random spawning now skips the high-speed variants, the 2-2-2, the 4-14-4, the 4-0-4 and every articulated layout by default (each can be ticked back on).
- diagnostics captures now record curves, grip and drag.
- the structural self-test now covers every arrangement.

Not changed
- still no Realistic Firing support.
- no multiplayer sync.
